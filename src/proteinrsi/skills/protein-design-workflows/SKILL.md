# Protein design workflow templates

Select a task-appropriate template before expanding its metric cards. Templates are method knowledge, not another execution engine. A may adapt or combine them, or choose no template for an explicitly exploratory plan. Produce the existing resource Protocol using actual authorized operations.

Categories: existing-protein optimization; fixed-backbone sequence design; de novo structure design; binder/interface design; functional-site/enzyme design; oligomer/assembly design. Ranking and prediction are supporting task modes, not extra design categories.

`templates.json` is the single source for short descriptions, steps, metric roles, dependencies, adjustable choices and sources. Missing tools/inputs and reference-only methods are disclosed separately by the runtime. Do not claim a published method was reproduced after substituting a different model or omitting its filters. The Virtual Lab, BindCraft and Claude references are distinct methods; their published weights and cutoffs are not universal defaults.

TaskProfile owns final outputs and hard constraints; A owns the current plan; ToolGateway owns authorization and execution. E/M adoption policy, measurement authority and historical studies are unchanged.
