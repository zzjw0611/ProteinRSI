---
name: experimental-feedback
description: Interpret experimental feedback without changing raw observations.
---
Verify sample identity, sequence hash, units and assay version before interpreting results.
Separate valid, failed and inconclusive measurements. A failure is not automatically zero activity.
Keep synthetic fixtures, revealed historical labels and newly generated wet measurements distinct.
Report prediction errors and alternative explanations. Do not infer a universal design principle
from one batch or let a favorable trial silently redefine the evaluation metric.
