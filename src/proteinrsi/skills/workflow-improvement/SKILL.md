---
name: workflow-improvement
description: Propose scoped, falsifiable modifications of research or meta policy.
---
Diagnose whether the problem is measurement quality, execution failure, or research strategy.
Abstain if evidence or remaining budget is inadequate. State the supporting trace identifiers.
Propose one bounded patch against the exact current workflow/meta version.
Never modify task objectives, labels, budget accounting, QC truth, permissions, or promotion gates.
A meta-policy patch must be independently evaluated by the quality of the descendants it produces.
Do not call a scripted demonstration proof of scientific or recursive improvement.
